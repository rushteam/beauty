FIG. 2 启动与生命周期流程

每个 Service 启动时创建两个 goroutine：编排协程（就绪 → 注册 → 关停顺序）和 serve 协程（实际运行服务），两协程协作实现优雅停机。

FIG. 4 — Shutdown Sequence
优雅停机时序
先注销 → 排空等待（drainDelay）→ 停服务 → 等 in-flight 请求 → After hooks，实现滚动发布零停机。
sequenceDiagram participant S as Signal participant App as App participant O as Orchestrator participant Sv as Service participant R as Registry participant L as LB/Client S->>App: SIGINT/SIGTERM App->>App: cancel(ctx) App->>O: ctx.Done() O->>R: deregister() R-->>L: instance offline Note over O,L: drainDelay wait
LB drains traffic O->>Sv: stopServe() Sv->>Sv: Shutdown(timeout)
wait in-flight Sv-->>App: Start() returns App->>App: svcWg.Wait() App->>App: AfterRun hooks
